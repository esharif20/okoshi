# GH-8: Paper material: Ink, Paint and Lift

Milestone: M2 The Diorama and the Stationery

The look of every surface, and the two Beats that draw and colour it.

## Done when

- One material serves all panels, with controls for Ink, Paint and Lift.
- In the Ink Beat line work runs across the paper; in the Paint Beat colour soaks in from the ground up with an uneven wet edge.
- Lifting a Room returns only that Room to Ink.
- Screentone shading is available to the material and as a full-screen cut.
- Low quality drops the costlier effects without changing the composition.

## Check

```
npm run build
```
