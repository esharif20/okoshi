# GH-11: Exploring the Rooms

Milestone: M2 The Diorama and the Stationery

The one live thing: point at a Room to Lift it, click to go in.

## Done when

- Pointing at a Room Lifts it, slides its screen open, lights its lamp and shows its Facts beside the Diorama.
- Clicking moves the camera into the Room and shows the Roles and Projects that belong to it; Escape or a back control returns.
- The same is reachable by keyboard as a list of links in reading order, with visible focus and accessible names.
- The pointer is an ink dot that opens into a loupe over the Diorama.

## Check

```
npm test -- rooms && npm run build
```
